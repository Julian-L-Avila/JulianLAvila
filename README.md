## 1. Profile Perspectives: Academic Research vs. Scientific Computing & Industry

### 🎓 Academic & Theoretical Physics Perspective
- **Spacetime Clifford Algebras & Relativistic Spinor Theory:** Coordinate-free reconstruction of the Dirac field via real Clifford algebras $\mathcal{C}\ell_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})$, the double cover $\mathrm{Spin}^+(1,3) \to \mathrm{SO}^+(1,3)$, even-multivector spinors $\psi = \rho^{1/2} e^{\beta I / 2} R$, Yvon–Takabayashi relativistic hydrodynamics, and Fierz–Pauli–Kofink bilinear identities ([Undergraduate Thesis Monograph](projects/01-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics/undergraduate-thesis-dirac-clifford/foundations-of-the-dirac-equation-thesis.pdf), nominated for *Mención Meritoria*).
- **Chiral Fermion Mass Generation & Neutrino Phenomenology:** Pseudo-Hermitian chiral mass matrix $M = m_1 \mathbb{I}_4 + i m_2 \gamma^5$ ($\eta H \eta^{-1} = H^\dagger$) generating seesaw mass splittings $m_\pm = m_1 \pm \sqrt{m_1^2 - m_2^2}$ ([Third Best Poster Award in Particle Physics, CIFI-SEMA 2026](projects/01-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics/chiral-dirac-and-neutrino-mass/cifi-sema-2026-poster.pdf); manuscript *"Hermiticity and Mass Generation in the Chiral Dirac Equation"* in preparation).
- **Relativistic Magnetohydrodynamics (RRMHD — CUEVA Research Group):** $3+1$ covariant tensor formulation coupling $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$ with Maxwell's equations and relativistic Ohm's law for astrophysical slab jets and resistive current sheets (Fortran 95 & Python; presented at **CoCoA 2024** and **XXX Congreso Nacional de Física 2025**).
- **Topological Phases of Matter & Semiclassical Asymptotics:** Classification of topological insulators/superconductors via Clifford algebra extensions $\mathcal{C}\ell_{p,q} \to \mathcal{C}\ell_{p,q+1}$ and Bott periodicity ([ISQSE 2025 Talk](projects/01-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics/topological-phases-and-jwkb/survey-topological-phases-iqse2025.pdf)), eikonal Hamilton–Jacobi to Schrödinger JWKB asymptotics ([XXX CNF 2025 Talk](projects/01-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics/topological-phases-and-jwkb/eikonal-jwkb-xxxcnf2025.pdf)), and the historical fragmentation of geometric formalisms ([III Semana de la Ciencia 2026 Talk](projects/01-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics/topological-phases-and-jwkb/guerra-vectorial-2026.pdf)).

### 💻 Scientific Computing, SciML & Industry Perspective
- **Hybrid C++17 / `pybind11` / Python Scientific Software (`dirac-solver`):** Open-source package ([`projects/02-computational-physics-and-software/dirac-equation-solver/`](projects/02-computational-physics-and-software/dirac-equation-solver/), MIT License, software registration pending) implementing staggered-leapfrog FDTD and spectral integrators for the (1D/2D/3D) Dirac equation with canonical design patterns (Builder, Strategy, Factory, Observer), HDF5 persistence, [Doxygen Reference Manual](projects/02-computational-physics-and-software/dirac-equation-solver/dirac-solver-doxygen-refman.pdf), and automated `pytest` tests.
- **High-Performance Computing Across 5 Languages:** Production numerical solvers in **Fortran 90/95, C, C++17, Julia, Python, and MATLAB**, including a [5-language 2D Laplace PDE benchmark](projects/02-computational-physics-and-software/laplace-2d-multilanguage-benchmark/laplace-benchmark-report.pdf) (`0.097 s` in Fortran 90 / `73.8x` speedup over Python), symplectic Velocity-Verlet Molecular Dynamics ([`DM.f90`](projects/02-computational-physics-and-software/molecular-dynamics-fortran90/DM.f90)), and 2D viscous pipe flow ([`fluid-sim.cpp`](projects/02-computational-physics-and-software/ode-solvers-and-simulations/fluid-sim.cpp)).
- **Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) & Data Mining:** Automatic-differentiation Physics-Informed Neural Networks ([Quarto PINN Suite](notebooks/pinn-damped-oscillator.html)), 2D Galerkin Finite Element Method solvers ([Quarto FEM Suite](notebooks/poisson-finite-elements.html)), and a comparative benchmark of 11 ML classifiers on `36,358` LHC collision events achieving `90.3%` accuracy / `0.958` ROC-AUC ([Quarto LHC Suite](notebooks/lhc-particle-classification.html)).
- **Model Validation, Control Engineering & AI Reasoning Benchmarks:** Analytical and experimental validation of numerical models via FFT spectral analysis ([Quarto Coupled Pendulums Suite](notebooks/coupled-pendulums-fft.html)), thermal step-response system identification ([`electric-furnace-system-identification.pdf`](projects/05-experimental-systems-and-engineering/dynamical-systems-and-digital-alu/electric-furnace-system-identification.pdf)) with C++ Integral Absolute Error graders ([`ieap.cpp`](projects/05-experimental-systems-and-engineering/dynamical-systems-and-digital-alu/ieap.cpp)), and multi-stage quantum mechanics benchmark derivations for auditing LLM symbolic reasoning ([`csco-4d-hilbert-space.pdf`](projects/01-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics/quantum-mechanics-foundations/csco-4d-hilbert-space.pdf)).
