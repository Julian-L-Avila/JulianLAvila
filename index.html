Foundations of the Dirac Equation: From Relativistic Principles to Clifford Algebras

A self-contained, coordinate-free reconstruction of relativistic spin-1/2 field theory from first principles. Traces the historical evolution and fragmentation of geometric formalisms (Hamilton's quaternions, Grassmann's exterior algebra, and Clifford's geometric product versus Gibbs–Heaviside vector analysis), constructs the spacetime Clifford algebra \(\mathcal{C}\ell_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})\) and the spin covering group \(\mathrm{Spin}^+(1,3) \to \mathrm{SO}^+(1,3)\), derives the Dirac operator by linearizing the Klein–Gordon–Fock operator over quadratic spaces, and formulates real even-multivector spinor fields \(\psi = \rho^{1/2} e^{\beta I / 2} R\) via the Yvon–Takabayashi hydrodynamic decomposition, Fierz bilinear identities, electromagnetic gauge coupling, and discrete \(C, P, T\) symmetries.

Reconstrucción libre de coordenadas de la teoría relativista de campos de espín 1/2 desde primeros principios. Examina la evolución y fragmentación histórica de los formalismos geométricos (Hamilton, Grassmann y Clifford frente al análisis vectorial de Gibbs–Heaviside), construye el álgebra de espacio-tiempo \(\mathcal{C}\ell_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})\) y el grupo de recubrimiento \(\mathrm{Spin}^+(1,3) \to \mathrm{SO}^+(1,3)\), deriva el operador de Dirac linealizando la ecuación de Klein–Gordon–Fock sobre espacios cuadráticos y formula espinores reales como multivectores pares \(\psi = \rho^{1/2} e^{\beta I / 2} R\).
