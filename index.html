Foundations of the Dirac Equation: From Relativistic Principles to Clifford Algebras

A 5-chapter coordinate-free reconstruction of relativistic spin-1/2 field theory from first principles. Traces the historical fragmentation of 19th-century geometric formalisms (Hamilton, Grassmann, Clifford vs. Gibbs–Heaviside), constructs the spacetime Clifford algebra \(\mathcal{C}\ell_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})\) and the spin double cover \(\mathrm{Spin}^+(1,3) \to \mathrm{SO}^+(1,3)\), derives the Dirac operator by linearizing the Klein–Gordon–Fock equation over quadratic spaces, and formulates real even-multivector spinors \(\psi = \rho^{1/2} e^{\beta I/2} R\) via the Yvon–Takabayashi hydrodynamic decomposition, bilinear Fierz identities, electromagnetic gauge coupling, and discrete \(C, P, T\) symmetries.

Reconstrucción libre de coordenadas en 5 capítulos de la teoría relativista de campos de espín 1/2 desde primeros principios. Analiza la fragmentación histórica de los formalismos geométricos (Hamilton, Grassmann, Clifford vs. Gibbs–Heaviside), construye el álgebra de espacio-tiempo \(\mathcal{C}\ell_{1,3}(\mathbb{R})\) y el recubrimiento doble \(\mathrm{Spin}^+(1,3) \to \mathrm{SO}^+(1,3)\), deriva el operador de Dirac factorizando la ecuación de Klein–Gordon–Fock y formula espinores reales como multivectores pares \(\psi = \rho^{1/2} e^{\beta I/2} R\).
